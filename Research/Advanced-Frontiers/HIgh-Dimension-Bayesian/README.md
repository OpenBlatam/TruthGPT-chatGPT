# High Dimensional Bayesian

Description: Research the High Dimensional Bayesian for optimization the generative AI.


Implementation:

Train a Transformer model to mimic or replace parts of the CMA-BO logic
e.g., Learn to propose candidates instead of running BO/TuRBO manually

Wrap the whole CMA-Meta-Algorithm into a Transformer-style pipeline
e.g., Treat optimization like a sequence modeling problem

Use this CMA-BO logic as part of a training routine for a Transformer
e.g., Use it to tune hyperparameters of a Transformer or act as an RL policy

Embed Transformer components (like attention) within the optimizer
e.g., Replace the GP in BO with an attention-based model
