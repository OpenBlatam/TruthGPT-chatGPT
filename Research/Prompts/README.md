# 🧠 Awesome Prompt Engineering

A curated collection of resources, papers, books, and tools on **Prompt Engineering** for Large Language Models (LLMs). Whether you're just getting started or looking to deepen your knowledge, this list has something for you! 🚀

---

## ✨ Description

Prompt engineering is the art and science of crafting effective prompts to guide the behavior and output of large language models (LLMs). This repository gathers the best resources to help you understand, apply, and master prompt engineering techniques. 🔧🧩

---

## 💡 Design Rationale

This prompt is designed to be clear, specific, and easy to adapt. It focuses on:

- 🎯 Clear intent: The prompt clearly communicates the goal to the model.
- 🧩 Enough context: Key information is included to reduce confusion.
- 📐 Structured format: Uses formatting to guide the model’s response.
- ⚙️ Reusable design: Easy to tweak for other tasks or domains.
- 📊 Easy to evaluate: Makes the output easier to review and compare.

The goal is to get consistent, useful responses from large language models.


This collection is organized by type—surveys, example-driven papers, books, social media insights, and useful code/tools—so that learners and practitioners can easily navigate and find high-quality materials relevant to their interests and needs. 📚🔍
